# Apple StoreKit External Purchase Entitlement (South Korea) 발췌
- URL: https://developer.apple.com/support/storekit-external-entitlement-kr/
- 조회: 2026-11-16 18시대 KST (개정일 표기 없음)
> developers of apps distributed on the App Store solely in South Korea can use the StoreKit External Purchase Entitlement to use alternative payment service providers for in-app purchases.
> Apple will charge a 26% commission on the price paid by the user, gross of any value-added taxes.
> The entitlement can only be used with a new binary distributed solely on the South Korea storefront.
> The entitlement cannot be used in the same app with Apple's in-app purchase system.
- 요청은 번들 ID 단위이고 "아직 App Store 에 게시되지 않은 번들 ID" 에만 부여된다(페이지 요지).
